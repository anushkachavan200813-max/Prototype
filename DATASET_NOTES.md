# Training Dataset Notes

- File: training_data.csv
- Records: 40
- Food types: 5
- Packaging materials: 8
- Labels: candidate_for_review and not_candidate_in_demo

## Data limitations

The current dataset is an illustrative demo dataset. The label basis is
not documented in the CSV, and the labels have not been verified against
experimental packaging tests or validated food-specific requirements.

The model output and score are for demonstration and preliminary review
only. They must not be treated as validated packaging recommendations,
food-safety assessments, or regulatory compliance results.

## Future improvement

Replace or supplement illustrative examples with traceable, reliable
data. Record the source, measurement conditions, and rationale for each
label before using the data for real-world recommendation claims.
